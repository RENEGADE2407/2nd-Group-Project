Member B - Campus graph and BFS
  - include/CampusGraph.h, src/CampusGraph.cpp
      * Undirected graph stored as an adjacency list
      * Loading buildings.txt and connections.txt, with validation, warnings
        (filename, line number, reason), and load summaries
      * Graph display, building/connection counts, and degrees
      * BFS reachability (queue plus visited marked at enqueue)
  - include/Utils.h: shared string helpers (trim, case-insensitive compare,
    comment detection, field splitting) used by the team
  - Own test data: testdata/graph_edge_cases/
  - Own tests: Section B of test_results.txt; testdata/graph_demo.cpp
    (standalone test)

Member C - Department tree and preorder traversal
  - include/DepartmentNode.h, include/DepartmentTree.h, src/DepartmentTree.cpp
    * General tree structure used to represent the academic department hierarchy.
    * Loading data/departments.txt with validation, warnings (filename, line number, reason), and load summaries
    * Department insertion, including root creation, parent validation, and rejection of invalid additions.
    * Duplicate department detection, including case-insensitive comparisons.
    * Department searching and then reporting parent, child count, and depth.
    * Hierarchy display using preorder traversal with four-space indentation for each level.
    * Preorder traversal without indentation.
    * Helper functions for whitespace removal, case normalization, and recursive tree cleanup.
  - Own tests: Section C of test_results.txt
